# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial coupled planar aircraft-arm model with sensor-derived control, measured-rotor-wrench reaction compensation, electrical draw, payload/contact events and 27 reproducible studies.

Separate spatial quaternion benches cover rigid-body invariants, attitude recovery and failed-rotor allocation. Full spatial arm coupling and hardware qualification are outside this release.

Archives retain physical settings, source provenance, raw arrays, plots, canonical exports and integrity manifests. Challenge cases preserve model errors and actual envelope termination.
